Interventional Cardiology & Hemodynamics

Cardiovascular diagnosis and treatment by catheterization

Hemodynamics is the area of cardiology that studies how the heart and blood vessels function through catheter-based procedures — access through an artery, usually in the wrist or the groin. It allows the diagnosis, and in many cases the treatment, of different cardiovascular conditions in the same procedure.

Procedures

Our services

Every procedure is indicated after an individual medical evaluation. The information below is general and does not replace a consultation with the treating medical team.

Diagnostic cardiac catheterization

A study that evaluates the condition of the coronary arteries and how the heart functions.

Coronary angiography

An imaging study that visualizes the coronary arteries and detects obstructions.

Coronary angioplasty

A procedure to clear a blocked coronary artery and restore blood flow to the heart.

Stent placement

Implantation of a small device that helps keep a treated artery open.

Invasive diagnostic studies

Other catheter-based studies to evaluate cardiovascular function in detail.

Interventional treatment of cardiovascular disease

Therapeutic management of various cardiovascular conditions using hemodynamics techniques.
