Our services

Every procedure is explained clearly, so patients understand what is done, why, and what to expect. Information is part of the care we provide.

Diagnostic Cardiac Catheterization

Evaluates how the heart and blood vessels function by introducing a catheter into the heart's chambers.

02

Coronary Angiography

Imaging study that visualizes the coronary arteries by injecting contrast and recording X-ray images.

See more →
03

Coronary Angioplasty

Treats narrowing or blockage of a coronary artery, with the goal of restoring blood flow to the heart.

See more →
04

Stent Placement

Expandable mesh device implanted in an artery treated by angioplasty to help keep it open.

Invasive Diagnostic Studies

Other catheterization-based studies to evaluate the heart and blood vessels in detail, when non-invasive studies are not enough.

Interventional Treatment

Groups together different therapeutic catheterization procedures to treat cardiovascular disease without open-heart surgery.
