Cardio Medicine

Clinical cardiovascular evaluation

The Cardio Medicine area at SAH handles the clinical evaluation of cardiovascular patients, before, during and after a hemodynamics procedure, along with ongoing follow-up over time.

Cardiovascular evaluation

Clinical consultation aimed at assessing a patient's cardiovascular status and detecting risk factors.

Guidance before procedures

A clear explanation of what the indicated procedure involves, before it takes place.

Medical follow-up

Ongoing monitoring of cardiovascular patients, coordinated with other physicians.

Pre-surgical cardiovascular evaluation

Assessment of cardiovascular risk in patients about to undergo another surgery.

Prevention and control of risk factors

Guidance on hypertension, cholesterol, diabetes, smoking and other cardiovascular risk factors.

Coordination with other physicians

Work in tandem with each patient's treating medical team for a comprehensive approach.

Every indication depends on an individual medical evaluation. The information on this page is general and does not replace a consultation.
