1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser to remember information about your visit, such as your preferences or how you use the site.

2. Cookies used by this site

  • Necessary cookies: essential for the site to function (for example, remembering your language preference or your decision on the cookie banner). They do not require consent.
  • Analytics cookies: help us understand how the site is used, so we can improve it. They are only activated if you give your consent in the cookie banner. [PENDING INFORMATION] — analytics tool to be used.

3. Managing your consent

When you enter the site, a banner lets you accept, reject or configure non-necessary cookies. You can change your decision at any time from your browser's cookie settings or from the link available in the site's footer.

4. Third-party cookies

If third-party tools are added in the future (for example, maps or web analytics), this policy will be updated to detail them. Currently, font loading is done through Google Fonts.
