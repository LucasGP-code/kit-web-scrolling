Introduction

Dr. Corina Biagioni leads the medical direction of SAH, specialized in interventional cardiology. Her approach seeks to combine the precision of hemodynamics procedures with close, attentive care: explaining each step clearly, answering questions, and supporting both the patient and their family throughout the whole process.

Every consultation starts by listening to the patient and explaining, in clear language, what the indicated study or procedure involves. The goal is for the person and their family to understand each stage of the process, while keeping in mind that every case requires an individual medical evaluation.
